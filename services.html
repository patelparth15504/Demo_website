Hidden leaks can cause thousands in damage. Our non-invasive detection methods — acoustic, thermal imaging, and tracer gas — pinpoint problems without unnecessary demolition. Trenchless pipe relining restores your pipes with minimal disruption.

  • Under-slab and in-wall leak location
  • Epoxy pipe relining — no digging required
  • Detailed report for insurance claims
