Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking — licencing, warranties, payment, and more.

Yes. Artisan Plumbing Co. holds NSW Plumber Licence #123456C and $20 million public liability insurance. We are also members of the Master Plumbers Association of NSW. Certificates of compliance are issued for all regulated work including gas fitting and major plumbing installations.

All our workmanship is backed by a 5-year warranty. Fixtures and appliances carry separate manufacturer warranties — typically 1–10 years depending on the product. We register warranties on your behalf and provide all documentation at handover.

For projects over $5,000, we typically structure payment as: 10% deposit on acceptance, 40% at commencement, 40% at practical completion, and 10% on final handover. Smaller jobs are invoiced on completion. We accept bank transfer, credit card, and EFTPOS. All quotes are GST inclusive.

A standard ensuite renovation typically takes 2–3 weeks. A full main bathroom with structural changes may take 3–5 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline in your fixed-price quote and send weekly progress updates. Factors like custom-order fixtures or council approvals may extend timelines — we'll flag these upfront.

Most bathroom and kitchen plumbing renovations don't require council approval if you're not changing the building footprint or structural elements. However, heritage-listed properties in suburbs like Paddington and Woollahra may require consent for visible external changes. We assess this during your consultation and can assist with documentation if needed.
