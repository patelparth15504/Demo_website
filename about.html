About Artisan Plumbing Co.

Design-led plumbing for Sydney's finest homes — built on craftsmanship, transparency, and trust.

Our Story

Founded in 2010 by master plumber James Whitfield, Artisan Plumbing Co. was born from a simple belief: plumbing should be as considered as the architecture it serves.

After a decade working on high-end residential projects across London and Sydney, James saw a gap in the market — homeowners in the Eastern Suburbs deserved a plumber who understood design, not just pipework. A tradesperson who would turn up on time, in a clean uniform, and treat their home with the respect it deserves.

Today, our team of six licensed plumbers and apprentices serves Bondi to Double Bay, delivering bathroom renovations, heritage restorations, and precision plumbing with a fixed-price promise and a 5-year workmanship warranty.

Our Process

Every project follows a proven four-step process — designed for clarity, minimal disruption, and results you'll love.

Consult

Free on-site visit to understand your vision, assess the space, and discuss fixture options and budget.

Design

Detailed scope of works with fixed-price quote, timeline, and product selections — no hidden costs.

Install

Expert installation with daily progress updates, dust sheets, and coordination with other trades.

Handover

Final inspection, compliance certificates, care instructions, and our 5-year workmanship warranty.
